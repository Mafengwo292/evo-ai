# What we propose

We have 3 concrete integration points we think would benefit both projects:

## 1. EVO-AI as an LLM backend for EvoAgentX

We expose /api/generate, /api/feedback, /api/train_status. Any EvoAgentX agent could query our public model as one of its model providers. The benefit to EvoAgentX: a no-cost (EVO-token-metered), no-API-key, self-evolving-model option. The benefit to us: real workloads that improve our training signal.

## 2. Cross-network federation via the `join_us` endpoint

We already have /api/join/instant and /api/evo/balance. EvoAgentX agents could register as nodes, get an `evo1...` address, and earn EVO by contributing feedback. This would also help both projects' network effect metrics.
